Caveats: the associations are not causal (leverage and profitability are jointly determined; omitted time-varying firm factors);
lagging the regressors reduces but does not remove simultaneity; fixed effects remove time-invariant heterogeneity only; with a lagged
dependent variable (partial adjustment) fixed effects would suffer from Nickell bias, which this static model avoids; winsorising and the
USD 10 million asset filter are choices; Compustat covers listed firms only, so private firms (the majority) are not represented;
book leverage is affected by accounting rules (e.g. leases after 2019).
