569 patients, 30 features; malignant 212 (37%), benign 357
Features are strongly correlated: 21 of 435 feature pairs have |correlation| > 0.9 (e.g. radius, perimeter and area measure almost the same thing)

Differences between the best models are small relative to the fold-to-fold spread, so with 569 patients the ranking among the

Note: because of the strong correlations, shuffling one feature is partly compensated by its correlated partners, so individual
importances are small and unstable even when the feature group matters a lot. Importance is not causal.

Caveats: one small, clean, curated data set from a single source; no external validation on patients from other hospitals;
the cost ratio is assumed; hyper-parameter grids are small; in practice calibration, fairness across patient groups and clinical
workflow would also need checking before any real use. This project is a methodological exercise, not a diagnostic tool.
