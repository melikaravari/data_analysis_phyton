Caveats: the GSS is a complex survey; the sampling weights, strata and clusters are not in this extract, so the standard errors
assume simple random sampling and are too small, and the descriptive means are not exactly population estimates; a test with 10
items has a ceiling and floor; vocabulary is an imperfect proxy for cognitive ability and depends on language background;
the cross-sectional comparisons of age groups mix age and cohort; education is partly a consequence of ability, so the education
coefficient is not the causal effect of schooling; the 'foreign-born' variable does not capture time in the US or first language.
