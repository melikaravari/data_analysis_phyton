Note: the 'placebo' test for pre-trends is not possible with one pre-period; the variable `nearinc` is also defined by a 3-mile radius,
so the treatment boundary is arbitrary.

Caveats: only two time periods, so parallel trends cannot be tested; repeated cross-sections with a changing mix of houses; the
treatment group is defined by a distance threshold; only 7 neighbourhoods, too few for cluster-robust standard errors; the 1978 'before' period
may already reflect anticipation of the incinerator; 321 observations give wide confidence intervals;
no check for spillovers (houses beyond 3 miles may also be affected).
