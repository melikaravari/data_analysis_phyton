Caveats: only 24 plots (12 per factor level) and 6 blocks, so little power for interactions; the three-factor interaction cannot be estimated because it is
confounded with blocks; agricultural yield data from 1937 may not resemble modern experiments; the p-values for six effects are not adjusted for multiple
testing; the effect sizes are for these soils and weather conditions, not general laws.
