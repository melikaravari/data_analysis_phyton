20000 replications per cell; Monte Carlo standard error of a rejection rate near 5% is about 0.15 percentage points.
So rates between about 4.7% and 5.3% are statistically indistinguishable from 5%.

Caveats: only a simple regression with one regressor and a few stylised DGPs; HAC with a fixed rule-of-thumb lag (no data-driven
bandwidth); no bootstrap or fixed-b critical values, which would improve HAC in small samples; the true slope and error
variance are fixed; results for other designs can differ.
