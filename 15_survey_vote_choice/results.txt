Caveats: ANES-style samples need survey weights and design-based standard errors, which are not in this extract, so the

coefficients are associations, not causal effects; income is coded as percentile groups; no interactions or non-linearities
beyond the single linearity check; no treatment of missing data (the extract is complete).
